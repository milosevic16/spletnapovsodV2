The stay

In the Karst and Vipava wine country

Brunarica Johanca is a charming 35 m² wooden cabin in the peaceful village of Gabrk. Surrounded by unspoiled nature, it offers a perfect retreat with a private jacuzzi, sauna, outdoor kitchen, and terrace overlooking forests and meadows. Ideal for couples, it combines rustic charm with modern comfort, including free Wi-Fi and a private bathroom.
