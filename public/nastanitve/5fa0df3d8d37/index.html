The Space

This spacious 100 m² two-bedroom apartment with a gallery can comfortably host up to 6 guests. The apartment features a bright 45 m² living room, where guests can relax or sleep on a sofa bed if preferred instead of the gallery bed.

There are two separate bedrooms (20–25 m² each), both furnished with 160×200 cm double beds. Above the living area, a gallery provides an additional 140×200 cm bed, creating a cozy and slightly elevated sleeping space.
