Kočevsko, the secret forest of Slovenia

Welcome to Kočevsko, a mystical forested landscape in southern Slovenia, nestled on a high plateau above the border river Kolpa. Vast forests cover nearly 80% of the Kočevje municipality, the largest in Slovenia, much of which is part of the Natura 2000 protected area. These forests are home to numerous animal species and large European predators — lynx, wolf, and bear.

In the Kočevsko forests, numerous free and organized activities await: hiking and trekking on marked trails, walks along forest educational paths, survival workshops in nature, mushroom picking, cycling and mountain biking descents, panoramic flights, horseback riding, and photo safaris. Guided bear watching and wolf tracking are also available.
