The stay

In Slovenia's alpine north-west

The house is very spacious. It has five bedrooms, a kitchen, two bathrooms and an additional - seperate toilet, a large living room with an exit to the balcony from where you have a breathtaking view of Mt. Triglav and Southern Bohinj mountains.

The house is 150m away from the Aqua park.

The house has 160m2 of living space in two floors.The bottom floor has a large living room that is equipped with beautiful early 20th century furniture. You can also find two bathrooms and a kitchen. On the upper floor there are four bedrooms and an extra toilet. The house excels in giving the guests a feeling of tranquility and well being.
