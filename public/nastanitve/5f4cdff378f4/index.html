The stay

In the Karst and Vipava wine country

Relax in this quiet and elegant space.

This is a house with history. Built in 1600, it is one of the oldest houses in the beautiful village of Šempas. It is located in the old village center, where the houses are in narrow streets and where you can feel the real rural atmosphere.

The village of Šempas lies in the heart of the Vipava Valley, known for its excellent wines. A starting point for many recreational activities. In less than an hour's drive, you can visit the coast, go rafting on the Soča River, skiing on Kanin, or take a trip to Ljubljana or Venice.
