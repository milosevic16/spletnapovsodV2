The stay

In Slovenia's spa country

Poldka Rooms are located in the heart of the town. The residence is distinguished by old bourgeois construction, with high ceilings and walls on which the past is restored. The equipment is modern, each of the rooms has its own bathroom, refrigerator, induction hob and all the utensils for preparing hot drinks. Guests have access to a balcony and a common area with charmingly restored antique pieces.
