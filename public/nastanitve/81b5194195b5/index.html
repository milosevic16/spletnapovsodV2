The stay

In the Slovenian countryside

The apartment offers everything you need for a relaxing and convenient stay. It features a fully equipped kitchen with a dining area and living room. The kitchen includes an induction stove, oven, dishwasher, and sink, allowing you to easily prepare meals just like at home.

The sleeping area includes a double bed suitable for up to 6 guests, and there is an additional extra bed in the living room for one person. The bathroom is equipped with a washing machine and dryer, fresh towels, and essential bath amenities. Bed linen is also provided.

For your comfort, the apartment offers free WiFi, TV, air conditioning, and a private parking space for one car. If you arrive with two cars, the second one can be parked approximately 150 meters from the apartment.
