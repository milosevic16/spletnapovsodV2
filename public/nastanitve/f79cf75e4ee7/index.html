A Stay Meant
to Linger

In Slovenia's alpine north-west.

Chalet Vrsnik Hideaway is the perfect retreat for those seeking peace, relaxation, and a true connection with nature. Located in the heart of Triglav National Park, it is surrounded by majestic mountains, fresh alpine air, and the tranquility of the Soča River.

This magical holiday home accommodates up to 6 guests, offering a cozy and inviting atmosphere where rustic charm meets modern comfort. Wooden details and carefully selected decor create a sense of warmth and homeliness. The house provides breathtaking views of the surrounding peaks and spacious areas for guests to enjoy.

The chalet features two bedrooms on the upper floor, accessible by stairs, with a total of three beds, including a double bed. In addition, guests have access to two bathrooms, ensuring comfort and convenience for everyone.
