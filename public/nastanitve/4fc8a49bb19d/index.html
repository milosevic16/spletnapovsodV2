The stay

In the Slovenian countryside

Are you looking for a place to stay for a short stopover or for a longer vacation? We offer both.

Our house is located in the small town of Prebold, surrounded by nature, but only 3 km from the highway.

We offer you an excellent starting point for cycling trips and hiking. Although we are small, we have a store, a bakery, a post office, a pizzeria, and a local pub in our town. In the summer, you can cool off in the public pool, stroll through the park with its children's playground and outdoor fitness area, or play a game of tennis or beach volleyball.
