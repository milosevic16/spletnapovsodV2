The stay

In Slovenia's alpine north-west

The apartment is a fully equipped unit in a complex (shared entrance to the building, separate entrance to the apartment), with as much as 150 square meters and three floors. The apartment is designed very thoughtfully and offers a lot of privacy, both when traveling with family or friends.

On the first floor there is a fully equipped kitchen with a luxurious dining room and a living room. The kitchen is fully equipped with all kitchen utensils, a stove and a dishwasher. The living room has a very comfortable sofa that can be stretched into two extra beds. There is also an additional toilet on this floor. From the living room there is an exit to a large terrace with a beautiful view of Lake Bled.

On the second floor there is a bedroom with two single beds. This floor also features a bedroom with a double bed and a balcony, which accommodates two single beds. There is also a bathroom with a shower on the second floor.
