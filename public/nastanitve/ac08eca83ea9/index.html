The stay

In the city

Hiša Urma**** is a modern, superiorly equipped house offering complete privacy, comfort, and peace in a natural setting – just 4.8 km from the center of Celje and 1.1 km from the Celje-East highway exit. Upon arrival, guests are greeted with a welcome surprise and have access to free private parking.

🏡 The entire 134 m² house is exclusively yours, featuring a private entrance and a spacious 20 m² terrace with a luxurious seating area – ideal for enjoying fresh air and peaceful moments.

Inside, the house offers a bright and elegantly furnished living room, a fully equipped kitchen, two cozy bedrooms, and a modern bathroom with an Adam & Eva bathtub for two and a walk-in shower.
