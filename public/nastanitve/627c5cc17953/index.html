The stay

In Slovenia's spa country

Welcome to your serene getaway at our apartment, a tranquil haven just 20 minutes from the lively Pohorje ski center. We're excited to host you and want to make your stay delightful and stress-free. First-time Airbnb guests, please touch base with us before booking. We're here to answer your queries and provide helpful info.

The pool is seasonal and available during the warmer months (exact dates may depend on weather). Please respect our house rules: no parties or events, no unregistered guests, and smoking only outside/on the terrace.

Early check-in or late check-out may be possible depending on availability – just ask us in advance. We’re happy to help with tips for local restaurants, thermal spas, hiking routes and family activities.
