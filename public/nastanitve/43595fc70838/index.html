The stay

In the Slovenian countryside

This nature retreat offers complete privacy and comfort without any neighbors or traffic nearby. The ground floor includes a cozy living room, kitchen, dining area, bathroom, separate toilet, storage room, and a spacious 45 m² terrace perfect for outdoor meals and relaxation.

The house is fully off-grid: electricity comes from solar panels, while heating is provided by heating panels. Tap water is not drinkable, but bottled water will be waiting for you in the cellar. Guests are kindly asked to use water and electricity mindfully. This home is not about luxury – it's about simplicity, silence, and connection with nature.
