The Space

Our rustic holiday home is cozy, comfortable, and full of character. The ground floor features a fully equipped kitchen with an oven, where you can prepare your favorite meals, and a warm living room with a TV. From here, step outside to the private terrace, the perfect place to enjoy your morning coffee, a glass of wine in the evening, or simply breathe in the fresh countryside air.

Upstairs, you’ll find a spacious bedroom with two double beds and one single bed, making it ideal for families or groups traveling together. If needed, we can provide a sixth bed on request. A bathroom with a shower is located on the ground floor, right next to the living room, for your convenience.
