The stay

In Slovenia's alpine north-west

Double Room Janez is a cozy attic retreat designed for two guests seeking peace and comfort in nature. The room features a comfortable double bed and a private bathroom with a shower connected directly to the bedroom.

Guests can enjoy modern amenities including air conditioning, WiFi, TV with Netflix, a desk, minibar, hairdryer, and towels. The shared terrace offers a quiet place to relax while listening to the nearby Belica stream and enjoying the peaceful surroundings.

Located on the attic floor and accessed by stairs, this room is an ideal choice for couples or solo travelers looking for a relaxing countryside escape.
