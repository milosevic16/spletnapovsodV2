The stay

In the city

Apartment Lilan is situated in the center of Maribor, 50 meters from the main bus station and 300 meters from the main train station.

The apartment is a duplex and provides free WiFi, a TV and a fully equipped kitchen. The bathroom is divided from the bedroom with a glass wall, both being situated on the upper floor. Towels and bed linen are offered. The apartment offers a private entrance directly from the street where you can park your car free at night and during the weekend.
