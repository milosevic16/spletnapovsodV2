The stay

In Slovenia's alpine north-west

Welcome to Apartment Brlog Petra! This cozy apartment is located in the heart of Kranjska Gora, providing easy access to the ski slopes, shops, and local restaurants.

The small but carefully furnished apartment offers a cozy living room with a dining area, a fully equipped kitchen, a comfortable bedroom, and a bathroom. There is also a storage room for skis and bicycles.

Kranjska Gora is a paradise for nature lovers. Enjoy hiking trails, skiing in winter and cycling in summer.
